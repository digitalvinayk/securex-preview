FAQ

Common Questions

How much does CCTV installation cost?+

Most residential systems start from £599 (2-camera Essential package) and commercial systems from £2,499. All quotes are fixed-price after a free site survey — no surprises on the bill.

How long does installation take?+

Most home installations are complete within a single day. Larger commercial systems may take 2–3 days depending on cabling complexity and number of cameras.

What areas of London do you cover?+

We install across Greater London including Central, North, South, East, West, plus Surrey, Hertfordshire, Buckinghamshire, Essex, and Thurrock. Free travel within the M25.

Will my CCTV work without Wi-Fi?+

Yes — our systems use local NVR storage so recording continues even if your internet goes down. Remote viewing requires Wi-Fi or 4G but local recording is always active.

Can I add cameras later?+

Absolutely. All our systems are modular — we install with future expansion in mind. Most NVRs support 8–16 cameras so you can add more as needed.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?+

Yes — annual maintenance contracts are available from £120/year, including health checks, firmware updates, and priority callouts. Commercial package includes this free for the first year.
